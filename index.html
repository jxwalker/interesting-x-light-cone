What you're looking at

A map of space and time in one object. The brass floor is space, and up is time. The glass cone is every path light can take from here and now. The glowing threads are two clocks: one that stays home and one on a round-trip rocket.

Why it matters: in 1908 Hermann Minkowski showed that space and time are one structure. People moving differently slice it at different angles, so they disagree about distances, durations and even which of two events happened first. But everyone agrees on the light cone and on one combined number, the spacetime interval.
